    About Plumage

    Plumage — by Gertrude Radio. A free, skinnable music player and visual engine for your browser, made by Redbird.

    Free. No paywall. No advertising. No account.

    Your music stays on your device. Files you open are played by your browser and never uploaded. No analytics, no trackers.
